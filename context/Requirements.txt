Functional Requirements
The system will provide the following functionality for customers and the salon owner.
Customer Requirements
FR-01: View Services
Customers shall be able to view the nail services offered by the salon, including service descriptions and pricing when available.
FR-02: Custom Nail Requests
Customers shall be able to request custom nail services or designs. The exact process for submitting a custom request, including whether customers may upload reference images and whether custom requests affect price or appointment duration, still needs to be determined.
FR-03: Appointment Type
Customers shall be able to choose between an in-salon appointment and an at-home appointment.
FR-04: View Availability
Customers shall be able to view available appointment times before booking an appointment.
FR-05: Book Without an Account
Customers shall be able to book an appointment without creating a user account. The booking process should require only the information necessary to create and manage the appointment.
FR-06: Customer Information
The system shall collect the customer's name and contact information when an appointment is booked.
FR-07: At-Home Address
For an at-home appointment, the system shall collect the customer's service address.
FR-08: Travel Fee
The system shall apply an appropriate travel fee to at-home appointments based on the customer's service location. The exact travel-fee calculation model still needs to be determined.
FR-09: Appointment Cancellation
Customers shall be able to cancel their own appointments. The exact cancellation deadline or cancellation policy still needs to be determined.
Administrative Requirements
FR-10: Admin Authentication
The salon owner shall have access to a secure administrative interface.
FR-11: Manage Appointments
The salon owner shall be able to view and manage booked appointments and relevant customer information.
FR-12: Manage Services
The salon owner shall be able to add, edit, and remove nail services and service prices.
FR-13: Manage Default Availability
The salon owner shall be able to define a default weekly availability schedule.
FR-14: Manage Custom Availability
The salon owner shall be able to modify availability when the regular weekly schedule changes.
FR-15: Prevent Double-Booking
The system shall prevent more than one customer from booking the same appointment time.
FR-16: Technician Schedule
The administrative system shall support management of the technician's schedule. The initial version of the system will support one technician, who is also the salon owner.
Nonfunctional Requirements
Usability
The website should be simple and easy to use, particularly during the booking process. Customers should be able to book an appointment without creating an account.
Performance
Pages and available appointment information should load quickly enough to provide a responsive booking experience.
Security and Privacy
Administrative functionality shall require authentication. Customer contact information and at-home service addresses shall be protected from unauthorized access.
Reliability
Appointment availability shall remain consistent between customers and the administrative interface. The system shall prevent double-booking.
Maintainability
The software should be organized so that services, availability, and other business rules can be updated without requiring major changes to unrelated parts of the system.
Scalability and Future Development
The system should be designed so that additional functionality, including online payments and email or text notifications, can be added in future versions.
Initial Release
The initial release will focus on the core booking system. Customers will be able to browse services, choose between in-salon and at-home appointments, view available appointment times, submit their information, and create an appointment without creating an account.
The salon owner will be able to manage appointments, services, pricing, and recurring or custom availability through an administrative interface.
Payment will occur at the appointment in the initial version of the system.
Future Enhancements
The following features are currently considered future enhancements rather than requirements for the initial release:
Online payment before an appointment
Email appointment notifications
Text/SMS appointment notifications
Open Requirements Questions
The following requirements still need additional clarification before implementation:
Custom nail requests
How will customers describe a custom nail design?
Will customers be able to upload reference photos?
Can a custom request change the appointment price?
Can a custom request change the appointment duration?
Travel fees
Will the travel fee use a flat fee, distance calculation, or radius-based zones?
What location will be used as the salon's base address?
Will there be a maximum distance for at-home appointments?
Cancellation
Is there a deadline for customer cancellations?
Should the salon owner receive a notification when an appointment is cancelled?
What information will customers need in order to cancel a booking?
